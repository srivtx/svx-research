We reduce risk. Starting with this offer.

‍

Most modernization decisions get made on faith — vendor promises, reference calls, analyst reports. We think you should be able to see what would happen to your actual system before you commit to anything.

‍

A Mechanical Orchard proof of concept runs Imogen against a real slice of your codebase — at no cost, under NDA, without access to your data — and gives you something concrete to make a decision with. And we do it in days, not months.

What you get

  • A modernization roadmap. Application inventory, dependencies, complexity analysis, integration touchpoints, with a sequenced, clear plan detailing the method, timeline, and cost to modernize the application.
  • An initial Java replica.  ~100k Lines of code characterization tested to show how Imogen creates output code and test harnesses
  • A custom demo environment. Your technical leads and business sponsors can see the results within a dedicated Imogen environment.

Once complete, you’ll know whether Imogen works on your system and how long a full modernization would realistically take.

‍

You can see what the first phase of an engagement would look like, to help you in estimating costs. You leave with findings you can act on with confidence, share it with your favorite system integrator, or help build your internal business case for modernization.

What you bring

Your source code for one or many applications and a technical contact who can answer questions during the engagement.

We handle the rest in our own environment — your code never leaves an MO-controlled cloud instance.

What this is — and isn’t

This PoC demonstrates that Imogen can replicate your system's behavior with fidelity; it isn't production-ready code you deploy on day one. If the results meet your criteria, we can move to a modernization program that produces a verified, production deployable replica of the application in your environment.
